In eighteen seventy-six, it rained meat in Kentucky.
March third. Bath County. A clear, sunny sky.
Mrs. Crouch was outside making soap, when something fell next to her. Then another. Then hundreds.
Pieces of meat. Fresh, red meat. Falling out of a cloudless sky.
They covered a field about the size of a football field.
The neighbors came running. And two brave men had an idea. They ate it.
One said it tasted like mutton. The other said venison.
Scientists got samples. One said it was lung. Another said it was algae. Nobody agreed.
So where did it come from?
You see, vultures fly in big flocks. And when a vulture gets scared… it throws up.
So the best guess, even today, is a flock of vultures, all getting sick at once.
Which means those two men had lunch… straight from a vulture's stomach.
