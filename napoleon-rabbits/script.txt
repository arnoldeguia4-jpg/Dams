In eighteen-oh-seven, Napoleon Bonaparte lost a battle… to rabbits.
He had just signed a peace treaty with Russia, and he wanted to celebrate with a rabbit hunt.
His chief of staff, Berthier, got everything ready. Hunters. Guns. A picnic. And hundreds of rabbits.
But here's the thing. They weren't wild rabbits. They were tame ones, bought from farmers.
So when the cages opened, the rabbits didn't run away. They ran toward Napoleon.
They thought he was bringing dinner.
Hundreds of hungry bunnies swarmed the Emperor. Up his boots. Into his coat.
His men swung their whips. Napoleon backed up. Then he ran.
He jumped into his carriage… and the rabbits jumped in after him.
At least, that's how one of his generals told it, years later.
The man who conquered half of Europe… was chased off the field by bunnies.
