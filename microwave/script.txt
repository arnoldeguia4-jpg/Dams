The microwave oven was invented by accident. Thanks to a melted candy bar.
Nineteen forty-five. Percy Spencer was a top engineer at Raytheon, a company building radar for the war. Not bad for a guy who dropped out of grammar school.
One day, he stood next to a magnetron, the tube that powers radar. And the candy bar in his pocket turned into goo.
Most people would just be mad about losing a snack. Percy got curious.
He held a bag of popcorn kernels up to the tube. They popped all over the lab.
Legend says he tried an egg next. It exploded.
So Raytheon built the first microwave oven. It was six feet tall and weighed about seven hundred and fifty pounds.
Today, there's one in almost every kitchen.
And Percy's reward for inventing it? A two-dollar bonus.
