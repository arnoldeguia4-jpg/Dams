Sweden built the most powerful warship in the world. It lasted twenty minutes.
Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. The Vasa had sixty-four bronze cannons, hundreds of carved statues, and enough gold paint to blind the enemy.
There was just one tiny problem. It was way too heavy on top.
Before the launch, the admiral ordered thirty sailors to run from one side of the deck to the other. The ship rocked so hard, he made them stop. After three runs. And then… they launched it anyway.
Crowds cheered from the shore. The cannons fired a salute. Then a little gust of wind blew. The Vasa leaned. Water poured in through the open gunports. It sank right there in the harbor, in front of everyone.
Three hundred and thirty-three years later, Sweden pulled it out of the mud.
Today it's one of the country's most famous ships. Because it sank.
