In eighteen fifty-nine, America and Britain almost went to war… over a pig.
San Juan Island sat between them, and both said it was theirs. An American farmer named Lyman Cutlar lived there. A British company owned the pigs.
One morning, Cutlar found a big black pig eating his potatoes. So he shot it.
He offered ten dollars. The British wanted a hundred, and threatened to arrest him.
So the Americans sent soldiers. Sixty-four of them.
The British sent warships. Five of them.
By August, four hundred sixty-one Americans and fourteen cannons faced two thousand British troops and seventy guns.
Everyone was ready. Nobody fired.
You see, the British admiral refused to start a war over a squabble about a pig.
So both armies just… stayed. For twelve years. They held parties. Raced horses.
In the end, a German emperor decided the island was American.
And the only casualty of the whole war… was the pig.
