In nineteen thirty-two, Australia declared war on a bird. And lost.
Western Australia. Twenty thousand emus marched into the wheat fields and treated the crops like an all-you-can-eat buffet.
So the farmers called the army. And the army sent one major, two soldiers, two machine guns and ten thousand bullets.
The emus didn't follow the plan. Every time the soldiers got close, the flock scattered in every direction.
They set up an ambush. The gun jammed. They chased them with a truck. The emus were faster.
The major said the birds could face machine guns like tanks.
After a week, the army packed up and went home. Then came back. Then went home again.
In the end, farmers got paid a bounty for every emu, and that finally worked.
So the mighty army lost the war. To a bird that can't even fly.
