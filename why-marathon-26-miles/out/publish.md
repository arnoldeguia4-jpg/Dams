Why is a marathon exactly 26.2 miles? Not 25. Not 26. 26.2. 🏃

It's not the distance the ancient Greek messenger ran. For the 1908 London Olympics, the marathon was planned at roughly 26 miles, but the organizers started the race at Windsor Castle, below the royal windows (the story goes the royal children wanted to watch from the nursery), and finished it right in front of the royal box in the stadium. That added an extra 385 yards.

Those last yards were brutal: race leader Dorando Pietri of Italy collapsed several times inside the stadium, officials helped him across the line, and he was disqualified after a protest. Johnny Hayes of the USA got the gold. The odd distance stuck, and in 1921 it became the official marathon length: 26 miles 385 yards (42.195 km). That's why a marathon is 26.2 miles.

📚 Facts: 1908 London Olympics, start at Windsor Castle, finish at the White City Stadium royal box; 26 mi 385 yd = 42.195 km; standardized by the IAAF in 1921. The "nursery window" detail is a popular account; sources differ on how much it shaped the route, and on how many times Pietri fell (four or five).
