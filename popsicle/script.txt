Why is it called a Popsicle? Blame an eleven-year-old who forgot his drink outside.
In nineteen-oh-five, in Oakland, California, young Frank Epperson mixed soda powder with water, stirred it with a little wooden stick, and left it on the porch.
That night, it got cold. Really cold.
The next morning, his drink was frozen solid, with the stick standing straight up like a handle.
Frank pulled it out and took a lick. Delicious.
He called it the Epsicle, and showed it off to all his friends.
Years later, Frank became a dad. And his kids kept begging for one thing: Pop's 'sicle!
In nineteen twenty-four, he patented it, using the name his kids gave it.
That's why it's called a Popsicle. It was named after their pop.
